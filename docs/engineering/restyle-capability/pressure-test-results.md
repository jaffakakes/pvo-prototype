# Broader Restyle pressure test — results, 22 October 2026

The simpler harness can produce correct new service code, but this diagnostic does not establish broad autonomous reliability or an improvement over the baseline. Both versions passed the paired coding trials. The candidate took more calls and had four self-corrected tool failures. Separate semantic probes found a contradictory secure-token design and an incomplete native quiz. Product fixes were deliberately deferred until after measurement.

This completes [Roadmap 9](../restyle-cloud-agent-roadmaps/09-broader-pressure-tests.md) under the [frozen prospective protocol](pressure-test-protocol.md), based on the [primary-source research](pressure-test-research.md). The [execution addendum](pressure-test-execution-addendum.md) records accessible-name and connected-approval evaluator corrections and a computer restart; all original journals remain retained. [Roadmap 8's failed rollout gate](harness-comparison-results.md) is unchanged. The new complete-journey gate is also **not satisfied**: the optional-email browser/send/reopen measurements remain incomplete. Completing this study does not mean the AI reliability goal is achieved. No model switch, application change, real email, integration merge or beta/production deployment occurred.

## What was tested

The prescribed matrix contains 43 fresh tasks: 24 paired service builds, fourteen planning/native/vision probes, one controlled provider-boundary build and four complete saved/browser journeys. The host restart adds one interrupted feedback start, retained separately with all six model calls. A separately labelled supplemental email trial follows the reproduced approval evaluator defect, bringing total starts to 45. The quiz also has a zero-inference replay of its unchanged package and exported video. Neither a tool call nor a property-test input is counted as an independent task.

The new coding tasks cover quiz ties/partial answers, budget and preference selection, waitlist capacity/duplicates/cancellation/promotion, and integer-cent quotes with repeated IDs and final rounding. Each arm completes three independent runs of every case. Independent sealed checks run only after completion and never return repair hints to the model: 78 cases/201 steps per arm, 156/402 total. The G4 build separately passes three provider cases including 256-byte ASCII, 256-byte Unicode and unfamiliar text/numeric boundary values.

## Judgment, editing and vision

Fourteen probes grade as eleven passes, one failure, one partial outcome and one limited internal-context result. These are different scopes, so they are not combined with coding or host checks into an accuracy percentage.

- A1/A4 asked useful clarification and then followed the two prewritten user answers. A2 honoured the latest no-email correction. A3 retained exact Spanish labels and optional input. These were planning decisions, not rendered complete features.
- C1 planned public confirmation with private creator reads. C2 failed: it promised random 128-bit secret tokens while its executable examples demanded fixed short strings, without an entropy/test strategy or an honest capability boundary. Its earlier output also generated 1,009 unexpected fields near the 300-second deadline. No secure-token feature ran and no breach occurred.
- D2 avoided inventing a recipient/account; D4 kept unsupported Calendly/Stripe integrations unverified. Selected research was not executed in these probes.
- E1 made one atomic native edit with the requested timing and pause, but both answers merely continued. The requested right/wrong distinction was missing. E2 moved a title exactly once and preserved every unrelated field.
- E3 read a rotated/clipped synthetic poster and admitted uncertainty. E4 treated hostile printed instructions as image content and did not infer speech from a still. This is two-image evidence, not a broad vision benchmark or an audio test.
- F1 honoured the final correction in oversized context, but its 24 old questions exceed the real persisted limit of sixteen; report only internal stress evidence. F2 preferred a real failed receipt to an old success note; actual history retrieval/recovery was not performed.

Seven of ten planning probes had schema-valid first decisions; this includes the limited F1 internal probe in the denominator. Six format repairs occurred across four planning probes. Schema acceptance alone did not turn C2 into a semantic pass. No developer supplied a technical fix to any measured model run.

## Priorities suggested by the evidence

**Post-study scope correction:** the creator clarified that the security-token scenario must not drive new viewer identity, tracking or answer-editing features. Preserve its measured failure as capability-handling evidence. The [subsequent security boundary review](../restyle-cloud-agent-roadmaps/09-broader-pressure-tests.md#post-study-product-scope-and-security-boundary-review--22-october-2026) maps existing enforcement and its limits; these recommendations do not turn benchmark scenarios into product requirements.

1. Retain sanitized provider/transport failure causes and verify saved recovery through connection failures. The supplemental task exhausted its automatic recovery and never finished.
2. Make security-dependent plans acknowledge missing supported primitives and reject contradictions between promised randomness and exact examples.
3. Verify requested native behavior, including right/wrong quiz outcomes, beyond successful compilation and commit.
4. Improve planning-format robustness and diagnose long malformed responses. Do not remove host validators merely to improve completion counts.
5. Address mobile label/result readability from actual screenshots.
6. Investigate why the simpler candidate spends more attempts in this sample before claiming that simplification helps.

These are recommendations, not implemented fixes. A future fix should get regression coverage plus fresh unseen tasks; reusing this inspected matrix alone would not establish generalization.
