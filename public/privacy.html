Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

This policy explains what Restyle handles when you use the editor, sign in with Google or email, ask its assistant for help, create a viewing link, or submit a reply through a video. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to support@getrestyle.app.

Sign-in and your account

Google sign-in lets us identify your account so you can export and manage links you create. We request only the openid and profile scopes. Google may return profile details such as your picture; we use your account's stable identifier and display name to create a Restyle account record. We do not request your email address, Google Drive files, contacts, or access to your Google content.

For email and password sign-in, Clerk handles account creation, password checks, email verification, recovery, and its own authentication session. Your browser sends your email address and password to Clerk through its sign-in component. Restyle does not save your email password or password hash in Cloudflare D1. Our account database stores a Restyle user ID, a display label, and Clerk's stable account identifier so exports and viewing links belong to the right person. Email accounts initially use a generic display label. We do not merge a Google account and an email account just because their addresses match; connecting them requires a separate, authenticated action.

Google's authorization tokens are used to complete sign-in and are not saved in your Restyle account. A signed, HttpOnly Restyle session cookie lasts up to seven days after Google sign-in or up to fifteen minutes after Clerk sign-in. A temporary cookie protects the Google sign-in flow and expires after ten minutes. Signing out ends the current Restyle session. Your account record and published links remain until they are deleted.

Projects and exports

Your editable drafts, imported footage, and edit history are saved in your browser's storage on this device. Signing in does not upload or sync them. You can remove local data using the browser's site-data controls.

When you choose to create a viewing link, the completed video or PVO export is uploaded to private Cloudflare storage. We store the link's title, filename, format, size, status, and account ownership in a database so you can manage it. A published link is unlisted, but anyone who has it can view or forward the export. Deleting a link stops new access and starts removal of its stored file; storage cleanup may take longer if a deletion attempt fails. It cannot retract copies already downloaded by viewers. A published export is a viewing copy, not a cloud backup of your editable project.

When a creator enables Collect replies, Restyle stores a reply box linked to their account. Answers submitted through that form are stored in Cloudflare D1 with the box identifier and submission time, and the creator can read them in Replies. Submit only information you intend to share with that creator. Trying a form inside the editor does not send a reply. A reply box and its answers expire 180 days after the box is created; the creator can delete the box sooner, which also stops new submissions from exported copies of the video.

Interactive videos may contain request actions configured by their creators. If a viewer uses one, its configured external destination may receive the submitted fields. Check the video and destination before sharing personal information through an interactive form.

Assistant requests

If you use the optional AI assistant, your request and relevant project context are sent through our Cloudflare Worker to a hosted AI provider. Depending on what you ask, this can include sampled video frames or short audio excerpts for visual understanding or transcription. The current production configuration uses Runpod for these model requests. In the editor, assistant replies and temporary working context remain in browser memory rather than your saved project; they are not added to your published export unless you apply an edit.

If the assistant searches the web, the search query is sent to DuckDuckGo. If it reads a public page, the requested website receives a request from our server.

We keep bounded operational diagnostics without storing your prompts, source, sampled images, audio, or transcripts in those diagnostics. To limit abuse and service usage, the server stores daily counters tied to a hashed client address rather than the raw address.

Optional iMessage test

If you submit a phone number on the optional iMessage test page, Cloudflare briefly holds it in a message queue so a connected Mac can send the test message through Apple's Messages service. The queued number expires after one minute if it is not collected. A keyed digest of the number and send attempt status may remain for about a day to limit use and show the result. Apple and the recipient's carrier may also process the message.

Service providers and retention

Cloudflare hosts the app, account database, published exports, and server requests. Google handles Google sign-in. Clerk handles email account credentials, verification and recovery messages, and its authentication session. Runpod processes assistant requests when you use that feature. Web search uses DuckDuckGo, and the optional iMessage test uses Apple's Messages service. These providers process data as needed to deliver those services and may handle operational information under their own policies.

Local projects remain until you or your browser remove them. Restyle sessions expire after seven days for Google sign-in or fifteen minutes for email sign-in; an active Clerk browser session may let you renew the Restyle session. Account information stays available for future sign-in. Published exports normally remain available until you delete their links, ask us to remove them, or we remove them under the Terms. We may retain limited operational records needed to run and protect the service. To request deletion of your account or other data we hold, contact support@getrestyle.app.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when our data practices change and show the new effective date. For privacy questions or requests, email support@getrestyle.app.
