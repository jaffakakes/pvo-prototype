Your account and content

Your content remains yours. When you create a viewing link, you allow Restyle to store and serve that exported copy so the link works. This permission ends when the link and its stored file are removed, although copies already downloaded by viewers remain outside our control.

Viewing links and interactive content

Links are unlisted, not private: anyone with the link can watch, forward or download the export. Review the result before sharing it. Interactive videos can include creator-configured requests to external websites; do not use them to collect information without a lawful reason and a clear explanation to viewers.

Do not use Restyle to publish unlawful, abusive, infringing or harmful material, interfere with the service, or bypass its limits. We may remove a link or restrict access to protect the service or respond to a valid complaint.

Availability and assistant

Restyle is a beta service. Features and storage limits may change, and access may be interrupted. Keep your own copies of important footage and exports; a published link is not an editable project backup. Assistant suggestions can be inaccurate, so review any suggested edit or answer before relying on or sharing it.
